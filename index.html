"We started Homefront Haven after a man we knew, twelve years in the Army, spent a winter in his car three miles from the base he served on. Housing is not complicated. People need a place that is theirs, a door that locks, and someone who picks up the phone. That is the whole job."

Dana Whitfield
Founder, and a veteran herself
For veterans

Homes for veterans leaving the street.

Permanent, genuinely affordable homes for veterans coming out of homelessness, with the practical help that keeps them housed for good.

  • Move-in ready homes they can actually afford
  • Help sorting VA benefits and finding work
  • Peer support from other veterans who have been there
For women and children

Safe housing, at an address we never publish.

Confidential homes for women and children leaving abuse. The locations stay private, and the support covers the things that actually matter when you are starting over.

  • Secure homes at undisclosed locations
  • School places and childcare sorted quickly
  • Legal advocacy to help you stay safe
