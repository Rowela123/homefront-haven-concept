What we do

Two communities. One promise: a safe, permanent home.

Housing for veterans

Permanent, supportive homes for veterans leaving homelessness — paired with the services that help them stay housed for good.

  • Move-in ready, genuinely affordable homes
  • Benefits navigation and employment support
  • On-site mental-health and peer support

Safe housing for women & children

Confidential, secure housing for women and children rebuilding their lives after abuse, with trauma-informed support every step of the way.

  • Undisclosed, secure locations
  • Trauma-informed case management
  • Childcare, schooling and legal advocacy
